Methodology and limitations

Administrative filters: LGA, district and ward values are read from the loaded EA GeoJSON properties. District options depend on the selected LGA; ward options depend on the selected district.

Spatial filtering: facilities are checked against loaded EA polygon geometry. A facility must be inside the national EA polygon coverage and inside the selected administrative EA polygons to be displayed.

Population: population totals are calculated from the selected population property. Missing or invalid values are excluded.

Accessibility: proximity analysis measures distance from EA polygon centroids to mapped facilities. It is not a population-weighted estimate, road-network catchment, or official accessibility measure.

Data quality: OpenStreetMap may omit facilities or contain outdated records. A mapped location does not establish that a facility is open, staffed, equipped or operational.

Boundary limitation: the union of the supplied EA polygons is used as the screening coverage. It is not an independent legal verification of The Gambia's international boundary.

Data sources and credits

EA boundaries: supplied Census EA GeoJSON.

Healthcare facilities: OpenStreetMap contributors, accessed via Overpass API.

Map: Leaflet with OpenStreetMap, Esri, CARTO and OpenTopoMap basemaps.

Routing: OSRM-compatible service via Leaflet Routing Machine.

Charts: Chart.js.

Results are for geospatial screening and research. Validate data before official publication.
