Methodology and limitations

EA population: sum of the selected population attribute over the filtered EA polygons. Missing values are excluded; the three fields are alternative estimates and are not added together.

Facility proximity: straight-line distance between the selected origin and mapped facilities.

Road routing: OSRM/OpenStreetMap road data when available. Fallback time is straight-line distance divided by the selected assumed speed, not a road journey estimate.

Coverage: OpenStreetMap data may omit facilities or contain outdated records. Verify locations against an official health facility register before planning use. This dashboard does not measure service capacity, quality, waiting times or emergency response.
