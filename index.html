Methodology and limitations

Administrative filtering: the application reads LGA, district and ward values from EA GeoJSON properties. District choices depend on the selected LGA, and ward choices depend on the selected district.

Population: the population total is calculated from the selected EA population property. Missing or invalid values are excluded.

Facility screening: facilities are retained only when their coordinates fall inside the loaded EA polygon coverage and the currently selected administrative polygons.

Proximity: the analysis uses EA polygon centroids and straight-line distances. It is not a road-network travel-time model or population-weighted accessibility estimate.

Limitations: OpenStreetMap may contain incomplete or outdated facility records. EA polygon coverage is used as the geographic screening boundary and does not independently verify the international boundary.

Data sources and credits
