How to interpret results

Proximity: the nearest facility is selected by straight-line distance, which may not be the facility with the shortest road journey.

Access screening: facilities within the selected radius are counted by geographic distance. This is not a population coverage estimate.

Data quality: OpenStreetMap may omit facilities, contain outdated records, or lack information on service capability. No facility should be assumed open or able to handle an emergency.
